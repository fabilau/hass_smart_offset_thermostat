# v2.0.6

This update makes room-temperature control more reliable and helps heating resume sooner after airing a room or using Boost.

Learning now handles idle periods and waiting times more reliably, reducing unnecessary delays in reaching your chosen temperature.

Restoring existing rooms is also more reliable with recent Home Assistant versions.

Preset selection, status details and error messages are now translated in all 12 supported languages. Smart Offset Thermostat is written out in full, while thermostat status labels stay compact.

Previously learned offsets are reset automatically once after updating. Your rooms then learn afresh; restarting Home Assistant will not repeat the reset. No manual reset is needed.
